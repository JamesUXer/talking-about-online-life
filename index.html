Talking About Online Life

Online safety CPD for primary school staff, grounded in KCSIE 2025, updated in September 2025, and NSPCC Learning online safety training.

This CPD sequence uses classroom-based scenarios to connect statutory safeguarding expectations with professional reflection on open communication about children's online lives.
