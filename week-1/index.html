Week 1

Online life is everyday life

For many pupils, games, videos, messages and online trends are part of everyday conversation. Staff do not need to be technology experts to show interest. A small, curious question can help make online life easier to talk about.

Children's online lives are not separate from their real lives. They are part of how children play, relax, communicate and explore. The first step is curiosity.

Guidance

Guidance connection

NSPCC guidance highlights the importance of talking with children about online safety and helping them know what to do if something online worries them. NSPCC Learning also frames online safety as part of a whole-school safeguarding approach.

KCSIE places safeguarding within the responsibility of schools and colleges. This microlearning series supports that wider safeguarding culture by focusing on everyday staff conversations.
